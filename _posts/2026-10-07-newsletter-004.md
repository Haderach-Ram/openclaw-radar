## This Week's Signal

### 1. Hermes Agent is outrunning everyone on raw growth

Between the 30 September and 7 October digests, hermesagent went from 250,096 to 251,718 stars, about +1,600. OpenClaw moved from 390,801 to 391,522 (+721), ZeroClaw from 32,917 to 32,937 (+20), IronClaw from 12,637 to 12,639 (+2) and Moltis from 2,877 to 2,883 (+6).

**What this means:** hermesagent is adding roughly twice OpenClaw's absolute stars on a smaller base. Stars are a weak signal, but a two-to-one gap that persists for weeks is attention shifting, not noise.

### 2. ZeroClaw is the busiest tracker by issue volume

The 7 October digest shows ZeroClaw with 12 new issues against 5 closed, and hermesagent with 11 new against 2 closed. OpenClaw opened 7 and closed 4. A widening open-issue count on fast-growing projects is the usual price of growth.

**What this means:** for anyone depending on these projects, watch the closed-to-opened ratio, not the star count. It says more about how quickly your bug will be looked at.

### 3. OpenClaw's release train is running on three tracks

The digests show 2026.10.1-beta.2 on 8 October and the 2026.9.9 stable line shipping side by side. Beta, current and maintenance branches all move in the same week.

**What this means:** pinning a version matters. "Latest" is not one thing at the moment.
