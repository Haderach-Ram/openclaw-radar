## This Week's Signal

### 1. OpenClaw shipped a pile of releases in one week

The 14 October digest lists 2026.10.5-beta.3, 2026.10.3, 2026.10.1-beta.1 and maintenance builds 2026.8.34, 2026.8.35 and 2026.9.8, with 2026.10.2 and 2026.10.5-beta.2 the day before and 2026.10.1 on 11 October. Two releases were reported on the day, and the 2026.10.x stable line moved from .1 to .3 inside a week.

**What this means:** that cadence is good for fixes and bad for anyone who upgrades by reflex. Read release notes before you pull.

### 2. Hermes Agent keeps compounding

hermesagent reached 253,516 stars (from 251,718 a week earlier, about +1,800), against OpenClaw's 391,918 (+396 on the week). It also shipped v0.21.6 on 9 October.

**What this means:** the growth gap widened. OpenClaw's growth in absolute terms is slowing while hermesagent's is not.

### 3. ZeroClaw: 12 issues opened, none closed on the day

The 14 October digest shows ZeroClaw with 12 new issues, 0 closed and 6 merged PRs. IronClaw: 12,653 stars and a quiet week. Moltis: 2,888.

**What this means:** a day with no closures is not a verdict, but with the previous week's 12-and-5 it is a pattern worth watching.
